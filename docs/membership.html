What does an Annual Membership get you?

OCEC's mission is to enable the attainment of equality for all through education, dialogue and advocacy. When you become a Member of OCEC, you are doing your part to see that goal realized in Orange County. An annual membership allows OCEC to keep advocating for students, monitoring the CA Department of Education, sponsor LGBTQ events, and present on LGBTQ issues all over the county! The annual membership also includes an invitation to our annual OCEC membership meeting + mixer. Join now!
